> **AI can simulate the feeling of connection without necessarily providing the relationship that creates connection.**

**AI → support → confidence → human interaction**

Imagine a commander activates an autonomous system, and the system independently identifies a civilian as a military target. The commander didn't select that individual, and the programmer didn't know the system would make that particular mistake.

**Who committed the unlawful act?**

That's the difficult question. Command responsibility generally requires knowledge or circumstances in which a commander should have known about the unlawful conduct and failed to prevent it. An unpredictable autonomous system can make that connection difficult to establish. ([Human Rights Watch][4])

> **Autonomy cannot be used to transfer legal responsibility from humans to machines.**
